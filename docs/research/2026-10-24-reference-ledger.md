## Mechanism coverage and disagreements

The reference pass covered rarity/acquisition, petal roles and stacking, durability/spacing, aggro/control, multi-part enemies, summons vs natural reward eligibility, resource recipes, collection goals, historical progression changes, and challenge modifiers. Current live drop tables, pity transitions, high-level XP, NPC state, multiplayer loot ownership and all contemporary videos remain unverified. Fanon/Fantasy/Better Florr variants, generic video aggregators and unrelated forum results were excluded.

The native game's current rules are source-authoritative for implementation. Community guidance is input to a proposal, not permission to change our account schema or economy. All new art in this increment is original procedural Canvas/CSS. Any future audio asset must carry an explicit license/provenance entry; generated or purchased sound is not implicitly cleared by these links.
