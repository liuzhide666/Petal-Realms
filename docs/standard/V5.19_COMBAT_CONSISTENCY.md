# Normative Supplement S3 - Combat consistency

## Scope and authority

S3 governs the V5.19 runtime changes and supersedes conflicting descriptions in earlier editions only for the behaviors explicitly listed here. The 138 registered mechanism identifiers retain their implemented, derived or proposed classifications. Save schema 5 and the account database remain compatible. No crafting, drop, critical, frost, skill-cooldown or event-weight coefficient is changed. Geometry and terminal-state corrections can change realized combat outcomes; they must not be described as purely decorative changes.

The official Florr entry page was directly observed. Its guest connection did not reach a playable world. Search verified the official game's combat-and-collection description, but community guides and third-party databases are not official formula specifications. This release uses original art and independent rules; it does not certify complete Florr parity. The original 347 manual pages remain frozen historical evidence, with their original source pins and limitations.

## S3-01: Terminal combat state

Let D be the death flag and H the player's health. Once a fatal event is committed, D=1, H=0 and pause=1. Within the current fixed tick, the implementation returns at the next explicit boundary after fatal world-event damage, poison, a monster special, body contact or a hostile projectile. Subsequent healing, retaliation, experience awards, friendly-shot resolution, collection and portal transitions must not run in that tick. The fatal source itself may already have changed position, visual feedback or its own cooldown before this boundary. This is ordered execution, not rollback of the entire tick.

For D=1, the ordinary update map is absorbing: U_dt(G)=G. Player and monster damage entry points respect terminal state. Only the explicit respawn transition returns the player to an active life. Respawn restores maximum health, clears transient effects and held input, resets equipment, clears transient shots and loot, snaps the camera to the safe position and restores canvas focus. Existing skill cooldowns remain on their previous simulation clock. Death does not introduce an inventory-loss rule.

## S3-02: Input ownership and probability-one transitions

Each captured touch pointer belongs to its joystick or action control. Canceling pointer p removes p from that owner's held set. Other held touch pointers remain active until their own release, cancellation or capture loss. Defense retains priority over attack. Blur, background transitions, menu entry, death and respawn neutralize all input. Non-touch pointer cancellation retains the existing global reset.

These are deterministic state transitions with probability one, not new random trials. A death/respawn transition must leave movement=(0,0) and action=normal unless a new eligible input arrives. A rendered pressed state must match the underlying held set. This ownership rule extends M003; it does not change joystick normalization, movement speed, attack radius or reload cost.

## S3-03: Damage acceptance and effective feedback

damagePlayer returns an acceptance Boolean A. A=0 for death, an active dash immunity interval or the existing 0.32-second body-hit grace; in these cases the projectile contact does not attach venom. For an accepted surviving venom hit, A=1 and venomUntil=t+3.5. The hostile projectile is consumed on contact regardless of A. Poison already present before a dash continues ticking; immunity is not a poison cleanse. Fatal contact enters S3-01 before any further status write.

For monster HP H>=0, requested damage X and armor B, mitigated damage is Y=max(1,X-B). The effective damage receipt is L=min(H,Y). Floating damage feedback reports rounded L; a batched exchange reports round(sum_i L_i). Crit count and exchange count remain separate indicators. The existing fang heal and lightning chain continue to use their declared requested-damage basis, so a feedback correction must not silently retune those effects. Negative HP from a prior poison tick contributes zero additional effective HP loss while the ordinary death pipeline records that kill.

## S3-04: Shared body geometry

Friendly projectile contact now calls mobCircleOverlap(m,x,y,r), the same oriented ellipse-circle predicate used by orbital melee petals. For a target ellipse with axes a,b and facing theta, rotate the projectile center into the body frame; an interior point hits. For an exterior point, contact occurs when the closest ellipse-boundary distance is less than r. The existing solver uses eighteen bisection iterations. A conservative outer circle remains a rejection shortcut inside that solver, not the final contact decision.

Wing, leg, tail, horn and other decorative extents remain excluded. A projectile beside the short body axis must not hit merely because it lies within m.r+r. A genuine torso contact still applies the ordinary damage pipeline and grape hit-ID/bounce budget. This remains a sampled fixed-step contact test, not continuous collision detection: very fast or unusually small future projectiles require a separate swept-collision contract.

## S3-05: Bounded progression

The authoritative saved-level range remains 1<=level<=999. The runtime progression loop consumes a next-level cost only while level<999. At the cap, experience is retained, bounded by the existing loader ceiling of 10^9. For earned experience E, xp'=min(10^9,xp+E) precedes the bounded progression loop. For levels below the cap, the existing requirement 35+floor(18*l+0.9*l^2) remains unchanged.

The last legal transition 998->999 remains reachable. A subsequent reward must not create level 1000 and thereby make the next cloud save fail validation. This ceiling is an operational compatibility constraint, not a claim that 999 is the maximum level of the reference game. All rarity, reward and slot-capacity laws retain their prior definitions.

## S3-06: Cloud-save retry state

The pending outbox remains owned by the same account and base revision until a matching successful upload clears or rebases it. HTTP 400,401,403,409 and413 do not automatically schedule another attempt inside the failed flush. Missing status,408,429 and5xx are retryable. For retry index k=1..6, d_k=max(1000*R,min(30000,2000*2^k)) milliseconds, where a missing Retry-After value gives R=0. Thus an ordinary transient failure starts at four seconds; a server's longer Retry-After is honored.

At most six retries are scheduled by that consecutive-failure sequence. A successful upload resets the retry counter. Only a confirmed successful upload may trigger the finalizer's scheduling path for a newer generation created during that upload. A fresh autosave or explicit online event is a separate trigger; this is not a permanent account lockout or a global lifetime request quota. Invalid or expired-session failures preserve pending bytes and show a recovery/export instruction. No authentication, account-identity or compare-and-swap boundary is bypassed.

## S3-07: Readable encounter presentation

All 29 loot families use the same original procedural petal outlines as equipment and inventory. Rarity remains a surrounding card border. Reduced decorative motion freezes optional loot bob and rotation. Rendering does not change item identity, collection radius, lifetime, rarity, count or gameplay RNG.

The guardian bar, event notice, player status and side tools occupy separate screen lanes. Portrait compact mode temporarily suppresses the biome card during a guardian encounter, leaving the map and guardian identity available. Short landscape mode narrows the central guardian strip. Desktop event notices appear below the guardian bar. Native DOM text remains separate from world canvas art. These layouts are tested at 1280x800,390x844,844x390,320x568 and568x320; safe-area insets remain part of CSS positioning.

## S3-08: Verification and remaining boundaries

The new combat browser suite reproduces terminal poison, meteor, body, nova and projectile deaths; held-input cleanup; accepted versus blocked venom; rotated-body projectile misses and hits; level and experience bounds; effective damage feedback; and encounter overlap cases. The retry suite exercises permanent failures, transient backoff, Retry-After, exhaustion and successful upload generations. Earlier interface, rendering, crafting and account suites remain required regressions.

These checks use isolated fixtures and Chromium mobile emulation. They do not certify physical iOS Safari, every touch gesture, all adversarial imported values, or production database availability. Native cloud accounts store client-authored single-player progress; server-authoritative combat, a shared multiplayer economy, full ordered replay, pity and general elemental matrices remain outside this release. Publication, runtime health, source identity and local verification are distinct claims. A source-pinned PDF is not proof of deployment.
