# PR-STD-003 edition 3.1 — V5.15 tactical atlas

The game keeps its 60 Hz simulation, schema-5 saves, existing drop rates and creature body colliders. This amendment makes conditional probability useful to the player and binds hostile previews to shared attack coefficients. It extends the catalog from M001–M124 to M001–M130; I/D/P statuses retain their meaning.

## Outcome-wise damage, M125

For one already-eligible melee hit, let X be raw damage after durability/talent/assault/overdrive, A current armor, H remaining target health, p the capped intact-Prism critical chance, and c=1.6. Set y0=min(H,max(1,X−A)) and y1=min(H,max(1,cX−A)). The panel reports μ=(1−p)y0+py1, variance p(1−p)(y1−y0)^2 and P(y>=H). Thus X=5,A=6,p=.15 gives μ=1.15, whereas flooring the averaged raw hit gives1. This counterexample prohibits averaging before armor. X=10,A=0,H=12 gives μ=10.3 and a15% single-hit kill chance.

`armorOf` and `rawPetalDamage` serve actual combat and UI. A row is conditional on that petal independently reaching the target once. It does not combine ten strikes, estimate orbit uptime or claim a damage-per-second ranking. The HP cap measures effective lost HP; the runtime may retain a negative post-kill HP value internally. Body contact, poison, projectiles and chain damage are outside this melee forecast.

## Exact target acquisition, M126

For shared primary rank r, let a(r) be primary-family selection probability at the exact requested rank, g(r) whether the fixed guardian channel guarantees the target, and b(r)=.18 if the optional Basic channel matches (otherwise0). The union probability is Σr P(R=r)[g(r)?1:a(r)+(1−a(r))b(r)]. This counts at least one copy, preserves duplicate-family multiplicity, treasure rank mixing and the rank-7 special case. It must not sum marginal target probabilities across shared channels. The tracked goal is exact family plus exact rank, including a truthful zero for unreachable ranks8–9. It is conditional on defeat and omits collection, travel, death and crafting.

## Full batch histogram, M127

The existing finite-stock dynamic program advances states (stock,wins), retaining ineligible stock<5. After at most5 attempts, h[j]=Σs P(stock=s,wins=j). All six bins0–5 are shown with percentages; Σh=1, E[wins]=Σj jh[j], and P(any)=1−h[0]. Success consumes5, while failure consumes a uniform1–4. This is a distribution, not a success promise. No new pity or random draw is introduced by analysis.

## Composable smoothing, M128

`alpha(g,dt)=1−(1−g)^(60dt)` preserves alpha(g,1/60)=g and `1−alpha(t1+t2)=(1−alpha(t1))(1−alpha(t2))`. Camera uses g=8/60; orbital pursuit uses g=.25. The implementation uses log1p/expm1 for numerical stability. dt0 gives0; g0 gives0; g1 gives1 for positive dt. The semigroup statement assumes a fixed target; it is not a proof of deterministic moving-target gameplay.

## Material, status and information hierarchy, M130

Anatomy classification matches Bee exactly rather than accidentally matching Beetle as a substring; Rat uses two leg pairs, ears and a tail. Clipped species materials use authoritative ellipse dimensions and disappear below17 projected pixels or in low quality. Regional vignette and shell traces are deterministic and consume no RNG. Frost, poison and shield use distinct broken-ring radii and pip counts as well as color. Charged attacks use progress arcs, rays/corridors, or radial fields according to behavior. These overlays write no combat state. The field atlas separates conditional reward moments, tracked-goal acquisition and collapsible per-petal contact forecasts. The craft panel shows the six-bin output distribution. When the desktop boss HUD is active, the redundant nearby-target card is hidden to keep the central view clear.

## Verification boundary

`tests/v515_rules.cjs` has seven grouped regression tests, including768 independently enumerated target-drop cases; it also checks floor/cap counterexamples, histogram moments, smoothing subdivision, actual profile coefficients and sniper lead. Browser tests use real HTTP assets and compare UI math to actual damage/projectile code, inspect nova drawing radius, verify analysis/render RNG isolation and four viewport layouts. Existing controls, account, crafting and creature geometry suites remain required. Screenshots are reviewed after panel animations finish. This amendment does not establish server-authoritative inventory, cross-platform lockstep or native Safari hardware performance.
